## 🎨 1. Asosiy Vizual DNK (Core Aesthetic)
Bizning barcha vizual materiallarimiz zerikarli stock turizm fotosuratlari emas, balki dunyoning eng oldi outdoor brendlari (*Arc'teryx, Salomon Advanced, Satisfy Running, Roa*) uslubidagi **kinematik, xom (raw) va jo'shqin** estetikaga asoslanadi.

### 5 Ta Majburiy Vizual Element:
1. **Shutter Motion Blur (Harakatdagi xiralashuv):** Odamlar yoki oyoq-qo'llarning harakatdan surkalishi (dinamika, tiriklik hissi).
2. **35mm Analog Film Grain (Plyonka donadorligi):** Sovuq raqamli tozalik emas, balki iliq, haqiqiy vintage plyonka fakturasi.
3. **Gorpcore Kiyimlar:** Yorqin xantal sariq (`Mustard Shell`), to'q-sariq yoki qora suv o'tkazmaydigan texnik kurtkalar, tog' ryukzagi, trekking poyabzallari.
4. **Haqiqiy Emotsiya (Non-posed):** Kameraga qarab jilmayish yo'q. Qoyaga tirmashish, shamolda yugurish, orqadan qarash (POV), tepalikka qo'llarni ko'tarish.
5. **Markaziy Osiyo / O'zbekiston Tog' Manzaralari:** Tyan-Shan, Chotqol, qorli cho'qqilar, archazorlar, toshli kanyonlar va feruza tog' ko'llari.

```text

```

```text
Raw cinematic editorial outdoor photography of [SUBJECT/ACTION IN UZBEK MOUNTAINS]. Wearing technical gorpcore apparel, mustard yellow and dark slate waterproof shell jacket with hiking backpack. Subtle intentional shutter drag motion blur capturing high dynamic movement. Authentic non-staged emotional moment, wind in hair, natural skin texture, 35mm film grain, Kodachrome vintage color grading, dramatic mountain ridge background with mist, high-fashion adventure aesthetics, photorealistic, 8k resolution, award-winning outdoor magazine shot.
```
